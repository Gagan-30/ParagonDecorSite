Our Story

Decades of Dedication to Your Home

Paragon Decor has been transforming UK homes for over 35 years. From a single paintbrush to a full team of skilled tradespeople, we've grown by doing one thing right every single time — delivering exceptional results with integrity and care.

Every project we take on is treated as if it were our own home. We show up on time, work cleanly, and don't leave until you're completely satisfied with the finish.

What We Do

Our Services

From a single room refresh to a full home renovation, we handle every aspect of interior and exterior decoration with expert skill.

Interior Painting

Flawless finish on every wall, ceiling and surface. We prep properly, use premium paints, and leave your space looking immaculate.

Exterior Painting

Weather-resistant coatings and meticulous preparation to protect and beautify your home's exterior for years to come.

Wallpapering

Precision hanging of all wallpaper types — from classic prints to feature walls with textured and luxury papers.

Kitchens

Full kitchen makeovers including painting, tiling, and finishing works to give your kitchen a brand new look and feel.

Bathrooms

Bathroom renovations and decorating — from fresh tiling and grouting to full suite fitting and damp-resistant coatings.

Flooring

Supply and installation of laminate, engineered wood, vinyl, and carpet to complete the look of any room.
